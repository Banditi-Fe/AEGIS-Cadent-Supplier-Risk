# Before a real supplier-risk pilot

This hackathon package is a local synthetic demonstrator, not a Cadent deployment. A real pilot would require the customer and system owners to agree on:

- Approved scope, named operational owner, target service outcomes and human decision authority.
- Supplier hierarchy and critical dependency data, provenance, update cadence and access rights.
- SAP Ariba Risk APIs and mapping, identity, least-privilege credentials, rate limits and audit requirements.
- ERP/inventory, demand, work-order, logistics and supplier notification interfaces.
- Approved data sources and freshness/confidence rules for financial, operational, continuity, cyber, ESG, compliance, geopolitical and reputational risks.
- Tenant isolation, SSO/RBAC, durable storage, encryption, secrets management, audit retention and monitoring.
- Recovery constraints, qualified-source evidence, safety rules, financial thresholds and approval matrix.
- Evaluation with historical incidents, false-positive/negative review, tabletop exercises, resilience tests and rollback procedures.
- Explicit separation of recommendations from transactions; connectors must be individually authorized, idempotent, monitored and tested in a sandbox before any controlled rollout.

Do not connect this prototype to production systems or use its fabricated estimates as operational inputs.
